# Kuiklo Knowledge Base (customer support)

## Q: What is Kuiklo and how does it work?
A: Kuiklo is a hyperlocal grocery delivery service in Patna. It delivers everyday essentials in as little as 10 minutes. You place an order on the Kuiklo app or website, and the order is dispatched from the nearest local store or dark store in your area.
Tags: about, basics

## Q: Who owns Kuiklo? Which company runs it?
A: Kuiklo is owned and managed by Perficient Marketing Private Limited. It is a Patna-focused hyperlocal quick commerce startup. Patna is its birthplace and home ground.
Tags: about, company

## Q: Which city does Kuiklo serve? Is Kuiklo available in my area?
A: Kuiklo operates in Patna only. It serves major localities in Patna, including Kankarbagh, Boring Road, Fraser Road, Kidwaipuri, Bailey Road, Rajendra Nagar, and more. To check your area, enter your location on the Kuiklo website or app. If 10-minute delivery is available, you will see active delivery slots. If not, Kuiklo says it is working on reaching your area soon.
Tags: coverage, areas, patna

## Q: Is 10-minute delivery really possible? How does it work?
A: Yes, if you are inside an active delivery zone. Fast-moving products are stored in nearby micro-warehouses (dark stores) within 2 to 3 km of your location. Orders are processed instantly and packed in under 60 seconds. Delivery partners are already positioned in your locality, so the closest rider picks up and delivers right away.
Tags: delivery, speed

## Q: Are there delivery charges on Kuiklo? Any hidden fees?
A: No. Kuiklo says there are zero delivery charges. What you see at checkout is exactly what you pay. No hidden fees, no platform fees, no surge pricing, no convenience fee.
Tags: fees, pricing, delivery charge

## Q: Is there a minimum order value?
A: No. There is no minimum order value. You can order a single bunch of coriander (dhaniya patta) or a monthly supply of rice.
Tags: pricing, minimum order

## Q: Are Kuiklo prices affordable? Why are prices low?
A: Kuiklo sources vegetables, fruits and staples directly from local farmers and wholesale markets. It stores them in local micro-warehouses and removes middlemen, so there is no retail markup. Kuiklo calls this wholesale pricing: "No middleman, no markup."
Tags: pricing, wholesale

## Q: What items can I order from Kuiklo?
A: Fresh vegetables and fruits, dairy products (like milk), snacks and packaged foods, beverages, staples (rice, dal, atta), cleaning supplies, personal care products, and daily household essentials. The catalog has 100+ fresh produce, dairy, staples and daily necessities.
Tags: products, catalog

## Q: Are vegetables and fruits fresh?
A: Yes. Kuiklo sources vegetables and fruits daily and runs quality checks before dispatch. Stock is restocked every morning. If any item does not meet expectations, contact support right away.
Tags: freshness, quality

## Q: Can I schedule a delivery for later?
A: Currently Kuiklo focuses on instant delivery. If scheduling is available in your area, you will see the option at checkout.
Tags: delivery, scheduling

## Q: What payment methods does Kuiklo accept?
A: Credit and debit cards, digital wallets (such as Apple Pay, Google Pay, Amazon Pay, Mobikwik), UPI, LazyPay, Paytm, and cash on delivery in select areas. Payments go through RazorPay as the payment gateway.
Tags: payment, COD, UPI

## Q: What is Kuiklo Money?
A: Kuiklo Money is Kuiklo's in-app wallet. It gives seamless payments without waiting for OTPs, zero payment failures, and instant refunds. The site also says a brand-new Wallet feature is coming soon.
Tags: payment, wallet, kuiklo money

## Q: Can I cancel or change my order after placing it?
A: Changes or cancellations are only possible within the first 60 seconds after placing an order, because of the rapid fulfilment model. Once an order is packed for delivery, it cannot be cancelled.
Tags: cancel, change order, 60 seconds

## Q: Can I return an item I ordered by mistake?
A: For health and safety reasons, Kuiklo does not accept returns of grocery items unless they are damaged or incorrect.
Tags: returns, refund

## Q: What if my order is late?
A: Allow a grace period of 5 to 10 minutes during peak hours. If it is delayed beyond that, contact support through the in-app chat.
Tags: late, delivery, support

## Q: An item is missing from my order. What should I do?
A: Inventory updates in real time based on store availability. If an item is missing, you can enable restock alerts or try again later. For a wrong or damaged item, contact support right away.
Tags: missing item, stock

## Q: I was charged but did not receive my order. How do refunds work?
A: Contact support immediately through the app. Refunds for failed deliveries are processed within 3 to 5 business days. Refunds go automatically to your original payment method. You can track refund status under "My Orders". Kuiklo Money refunds are instant.
Tags: refund, payment, charged

## Q: How do I contact Kuiklo customer service?
A: Tap "Help & Support" in the app and start a chat, or email support@kuiklo.com. Support usually replies within 5 to 10 minutes during business hours. Kuiklo has a Patna-based customer service team.
Tags: support, contact, email

## Q: How do I order groceries on Kuiklo?
A: Open the Kuiklo app or website, enter your delivery address, browse or search for items, add them to the cart, and confirm the order with a secure checkout. If you are in the service area, groceries arrive within minutes. You can track the order live in the app.
Tags: ordering, how to

## Q: Where can I download the Kuiklo app?
A: Android: Google Play, package `com.kuiklo.app`. iPhone: App Store, app id 6748861544 (apps.apple.com/in/app/kuiklo/id6748861544). You can also order on the website kuiklo.com.
Tags: app, download

## Q: Does Kuiklo have a referral program?
A: Yes. Invite friends to Kuiklo. You earn ₹50 when a friend places their first order, and the friend gets ₹50 instantly. Log in to the app to get your referral code.
Tags: refer, earn, rewards

## Q: What is Kuiklo Lit?
A: Kuiklo Lit is a separate 18+ app for age-restricted products: lighters, hookah needs, paan, and tobacco items behind an age check. It is distributed from the Kuiklo website, not the Play Store. Same fast delivery, same account, and your orders, wallet and addresses carry over. Caution: tobacco products are injurious to health.
Tags: kuiklo lit, 18+, paan

## Q: Does Kuiklo offer printing?
A: Yes. Print on Demand: upload a document, photo or ID card in the Kuiklo app, pick colour or black and white, paper size and copies. The nearest store prints it and delivers in minutes. Pay per page, no subscriptions, no hidden fees. Files are used only to fulfil the order, then discarded.
Tags: print, print on demand

## Q: Is my personal data secure? What data does Kuiklo collect?
A: Kuiklo says it uses industry-standard encryption and does not share data without consent. It collects name, email, phone, address, date of birth, device and location data (with permission). Data is stored on cloud infrastructure such as AWS. Data may be shared with vendors like SMS providers, payment gateways (RazorPay) and logistics partners. See the Privacy Policy on kuiklo.com.
Tags: privacy, data

## Q: How do I delete my Kuiklo account?
A: In the app go to Settings > Account > Delete Account and submit a deletion request. Your data is removed within about 7 business days (the account screen says 3 working days). Deleting removes order history, saved addresses and wishlist, and any store credits are lost. This cannot be undone.
Tags: account, delete, privacy

## Q: What are the age rules for using Kuiklo?
A: The Terms say you must be over 18 to use the service. The services are not directed at children under 13.
Tags: terms, age

## Q: Which law governs Kuiklo's terms?
A: The Terms are governed by the laws of India and the State of Bihar. Disputes should first be raised informally with the company. The Privacy Policy names the Courts of Kolkata for disputes. Terms last updated 19 November 2025.
Tags: terms, legal

## Q: Why is Kuiklo different from other quick commerce apps (Blinkit, Zepto, Instamart)?
A: Kuiklo's own claims: hyperlocal model with faster delivery in focused zones, better local stock availability, stronger connection with neighbourhood demand, zero delivery charges, no platform fees, wholesale prices, and a Patna-first team that knows local roads. It says customers on other apps pay up to 20 to 30% more for convenience.
Tags: comparison, competitors

## Q: What do customers say about Kuiklo?
A: The site shows a 4.9/5 rating and "loved by 15,000+ customers in Patna". Example reviews: an order of potatoes and dal arrived in 8 minutes in Kankarbagh; vegetables are fresh; no convenience fee; no minimum order. These are the company's chosen testimonials (Tier A for "they say it", not independent proof).
Tags: reviews, rating

## Q: What is the app having issues (crashes, slow loading)?
A: Try updating the app, clearing cache, or restarting your device. If it still fails, contact support through the Help section.
Tags: app, troubleshooting

## Q: Does Kuiklo deliver late at night?
A: The site targets "late night grocery delivery Patna" and says delivery depends on store hours and location. If service is active in your area you will see slots. Exact hours are not published.
Tags: hours, late night
