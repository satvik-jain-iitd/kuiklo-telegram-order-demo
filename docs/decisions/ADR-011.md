# ADR-011: Oracle box under pm2, not Render or Railway.

Status: accepted, 2026-10-08

Context: the STT, KB and voice service needs whisper (CT2), MiniLM (torch) and a connection
to local Ollama. Render and Railway free tiers were considered. They sleep when idle and
have too little RAM for whisper plus MiniLM plus torch. Decision: run `stt_server.py` as pm2
process `bhavna-stt` on the Oracle box (aarch64, 4 CPU, 22 GB RAM), the same box as n8n.
pm2 is saved and systemd-enabled so the service comes back after a reboot. It listens on
127.0.0.1 only. Trade-off: one box to keep alive; a restart drops any voice note in flight
(seen once as "connection refused"). Consequence: no cold starts, no network hop between
n8n and the service, secrets stay on one machine.
