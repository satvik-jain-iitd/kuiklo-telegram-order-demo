# ADR-007: FAQ bot with MiniLM retrieval and gemma3:4b on local Ollama.

Status: accepted, 2026-10-08

Context: questions that are not about the current order ("delivery charge kitna hai?")
need answers from Kuiklo's own site text. Decision: `docs/kb/kuiklo_kb.md` holds 31
`## Q:` chunks pulled from kuiklo.com. `kb.py` embeds them with all-MiniLM-L6-v2, takes
the top 3 by cosine, refuses under `MIN_SCORE` 0.30, and asks local Ollama for a short
Hinglish answer from those chunks only. If Ollama fails, the best chunk is sent as is.
Model: gemma3:4b, the owner's choice of quality over speed. Trade-off: 24 to 45 s per
answer on 4 CPU. gemma3:1b was 6 to 16 s but mostly English and sometimes garbled.
Consequence: good answers, slow chat. `KB_LLM_MODEL` env var switches the model;
qwen2.5:3b is the next test.
